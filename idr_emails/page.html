Collect subscriber emails

Turn a zip of contact lists into one CSV, ready to upload to the newsletter list. Duplicates are removed and names are filled in where the files have them.

English and Hindi lists are processed separately, one zip at a time.
A .zip with folders of .csv, .xlsx or .docx files. Any other files are skipped and listed in the result.
If left unticked, possible typos are only listed in a report.
